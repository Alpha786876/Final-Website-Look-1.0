How We Work

A clear path from
idea to space.

This is a generic design approach for this static presentation, not a claim about a fixed official studio process.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Start with the space, requirements and the direction you want to explore.

  2. 02

    Understanding the Space

    Consider proportions, movement, function, existing conditions and priorities.

  3. 03

    Concept & Design

    Shape the visual language through planning, materials, lighting, furniture and form.

  4. 04

    Refinement

    Develop the chosen direction with focused adjustments to details and composition.

  5. 05

    Execution Coordination

    Carry the design intent forward with attention to the details that make the space feel complete.
